Versado turns learning into something you do with your hands. Instead of reading a wall of text, you move the idea yourself, predict what happens, and see exactly where your thinking went right or wrong.

HOW A LESSON WORKS
• See – every concept starts as a picture, with no jargon or formulas.
• Explore – drag one or two controls and watch the board respond instantly.
• Predict – commit to an answer before the reveal. A wrong guess shows you the exact gap, not just "incorrect".
• Write it – only then does the formula appear, colour-matched to the parts you just moved.

REVIEWS THAT ARRIVE ON TIME
Lessons you finish come back as quick prediction checks, spaced out so they return just as you're about to forget, and mixed so you learn which idea fits which problem.

WHY "VERSADO"?
In Portuguese and Spanish, "versado" means well-versed: someone who knows a subject inside out. It comes from the Latin "versari", to be engaged with something, which comes from "versare", to turn over and over. That is the idea behind the app: you really know an idea once you have turned it over yourself.

The app is available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French and German. Lessons are in English for now.
